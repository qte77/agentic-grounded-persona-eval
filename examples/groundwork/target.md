# Target — Groundwork (sortmy.london)

This is the filled-in `config/target.md` used to produce this worked example — kept here as a
record of what was configured, since the live copy at `config/target.md` is gitignored scratch
space regenerated per run.

## Product under evaluation

- **Name:** Groundwork
- **Live URL:** https://sortmy.london
- **Slug:** groundwork

## Assumed ICPs

- Londoners who need to navigate a specific civic/public service but don't know where to start
  (e.g. finding the right NHS service for a non-emergency need without knowing the system)
- Newcomers or budget-conscious residents/visitors looking for free things to do in London (heritage
  sites, green spaces) without already knowing the city
- People about to hand money or personal details to an unfamiliar firm and wanting a quick sanity
  check that it isn't a scam/clone before proceeding
- Someone deciding whether to eat at a specific place and wanting its food hygiene rating before
  going in, not after

## Research constraints

- Scope research to the product's civic-wayfinding workflows (Sort My Care / Sort My Wander / Sort My
  Food Hygiene / Sort My Scam Check) — this is what the product's own README calls "the product"
  (single-input civic wayfinder). The bundled Founder's Copilot (funding-discovery for founders) is a
  distinct secondary workflow with its own separate ICP; left out of this run to keep the example
  focused rather than diluting evidence across two unrelated audiences.
- Geographic scope: London, UK. Prefer UK-specific sources/subreddits (r/london, r/AskUK,
  r/CasualUK) over generic startup-community sources, since the ICP here is "general public trying to
  navigate London," not "tech industry."
